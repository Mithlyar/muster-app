Muster privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Muster is a free, unofficial army list builder for Warhammer 40,000, made by one person as a hobby project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Games Workshop. This page says what Muster does with your data. In short: your lists stay on your devices, and, if you turn on sync, in a private folder of your own Google Drive. Muster has no server of its own and no account system.

What stays on your device

Your army lists, unit presets, colours, display settings and game tracker live in the app's storage on your device (the browser's storage for the website). Muster never sends them anywhere unless you share a list code yourself or turn on Google Drive sync.

Game data downloads

To stay up to date, Muster downloads game data (datasheets, points, missions) from a public GitHub repository. Like any download, this tells GitHub your IP address; nothing about you or your lists is sent. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that request.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google to sync your lists between devices:

  • Muster asks only for the drive.appdata permission: a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only Muster can use. It cannot see, change or delete any of your other files.
  • In that folder it keeps one file with your lists, unit presets and colours, and reads it back on your other devices.
  • The sign-in gives your device a token to reach that folder. It is kept on your device only, and is never sent to anyone but Google. Muster does not read your name, email address or contacts beyond what Google shows on the sign-in screen.
  • Sign out in Muster to stop syncing. To delete the synced file, remove Muster's app data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps → Muster → Delete hidden app data), or revoke Muster's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Muster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No tracking

Muster has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own, and sells or shares no data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Mithlyar/muster-app.
